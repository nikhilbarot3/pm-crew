TURBOFAN ENGINE MAINTENANCE MANUAL — SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA
=========================================================
⚠️  This document is SYNTHETIC data created for the PM Crew hackathon demo.
     It does NOT represent a real product or manufacturer.

SECTION 1: FAN / LOW-PRESSURE COMPRESSOR (Fan/LPC) ASSEMBLY
-------------------------------------------------------------
The Fan/LPC assembly is the first stage of the turbofan. It compresses incoming
air and splits flow between the core and bypass duct.

Common failure indicators:
- Elevated T24 (LPC outlet temperature) trending more than 8°C above baseline
- Nf (fan speed) dropping more than 2% under constant throttle
- Increased BPR variance across operating cycles

Recommended inspection interval: every 2,000 flight cycles or when RUL < 30.

Inspection procedure:
1. Remove fan cowl panels (see Section 4).
2. Inspect leading edges of fan blades for FOD nicks or corrosion.
3. Check LPC stator vanes for cracking using dye-penetrant method.
4. Measure tip clearances; reject if gap exceeds 0.045 in.
5. Lubricate fan bearings per lubrication schedule (Section 7).

Parts typically required:
- Fan blade set (Part# FB-2240-SET) — lead time 3–5 days
- LPC stator vane kit (Part# LPC-SV-KIT) — usually in stock
- Fan bearing set (Part# FBG-1180) — lead time 7–10 days

SECTION 2: HIGH-PRESSURE COMPRESSOR / CORE (HPC/Core)
------------------------------------------------------
The HPC further compresses air before combustion. Core faults are higher risk
because they affect combustion stability.

Common failure indicators:
- T30 (HPC outlet temperature) > 1,620 K sustained
- P30 (HPC outlet pressure) dropping > 5% from regime baseline
- Nc (core speed) fluctuations of ±1.5% or more

Recommended inspection interval: every 1,500 flight cycles or when RUL < 25.

Inspection procedure:
1. Borescope inspection through HPC access ports (Ports 3A, 3B, 3C).
2. Check compressor blades for tip rub, erosion, or cracks.
3. Inspect combustion liner for hot spots or burn-through.
4. Verify fuel nozzle spray patterns against reference card.

Parts typically required:
- HPC blade set (Part# HPC-BL-STAGE3) — lead time 5–7 days
- Combustion liner (Part# COMB-LNR-01) — lead time 10–14 days
- Fuel nozzle set (Part# FN-SET-12) — usually in stock

SECTION 3: TURBINE SECTION
--------------------------
The turbine extracts energy from the combustion gas to drive the compressor and fan.
Turbine section faults develop slowly but accelerate near end of life.

Common failure indicators:
- T50 (turbine exit temperature) increasing trend > 10 K above regime mean
- W31/W32 (bleed air flows) deviation > 3% from nominal
- Gradual decline in thermal efficiency index

Recommended inspection interval: every 1,800 flight cycles or when RUL < 40.

Inspection procedure:
1. Borescope inspection through turbine access ports (Ports 5A, 5B).
2. Inspect HPT and LPT blades for oxidation, cracking, or spallation of TBC.
3. Check turbine seals for wear or burn marks.
4. Verify turbine case temperatures are within limits.

Parts typically required:
- HPT blade set (Part# HPT-BL-S1) — lead time 14–21 days (order early)
- LPT blade set (Part# LPT-BL-S2) — lead time 7–10 days
- Turbine seal kit (Part# TSK-003) — usually in stock
